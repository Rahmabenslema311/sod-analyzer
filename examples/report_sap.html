Method and limitations

Effective permissions are the union of all assigned roles. A rule fires when all its permissions are present (and, where required, share an organizational unit). Compensating controls can lower severity but never raise it; expired controls revert. Rules, permission names and risk-score weights are illustrative and must be validated against the organization's processes and risk policy before any real use.
